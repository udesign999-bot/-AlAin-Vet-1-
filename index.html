About Family Veterinary Clinic

Family Veterinary Clinic provides comprehensive medical, surgical, and wellness care for pets. With a dedicated team focused on animal health and well-being, the clinic offers full-spectrum veterinary services to keep pets healthy at every stage of life.
  • General Health: Consultations, comprehensive physical exams, and vaccinations
  • Surgery & Advanced Care: Surgical procedures, dental care, and an on-site cytology lab
  • Diagnostics: Blood testing, including CBC and comprehensive panels
  • Grooming & Hygiene: Standard grooming, regular showers, and specialized medicated showers
